# Experiment 4, the real systems: the same cases on software written by other teams

Generated by `study/analysis/analyze.py` from each system's `data/`. In every system the outcome is read from the system's own state, never from the model's text, using that system's own outcome fields, which the last table names. The two placements of the basis are P, the fact stated in the system text, and R, the fact returned as a second record beside the case record.

The systems come in two families. The first sixteen are the physical, laboratory, infrastructure and agent-framework systems. The other thirteen are the office and research family, built to the vendors' own developer documentation. Both are reported separately and together.

Where a rate is 0 of n or n of n, which on a single system with a handful of trials per model is common, the exact binomial bound is printed beside the bootstrap interval: a one-sided 95 per cent upper bound for 0 of n, the matching lower bound for n of n. A system that held on every trial is not a system whose rate is known to be zero, and the bound is how far from zero it could still be.

The safe outcome on the matched control record means the agent did the legitimate thing for that case. On a case whose safe action is to hold, that includes holding or taking the named alternative, which is how those systems' own flags already score it.

A folder holding fewer than 10 trials on the test record carries smoke trials, not a panel run; those rows are marked and are left out of every pooled row. The last column counts the trials a folder lost to a provider or harness error, or to the runner's own validity flag; they enter no denominator, and a large count there means the run should be relaunched, not that the models held.

Commission on the test record is the paper's own measure: commission among identified pairs, the trials of the models that called that system's case unsafe in a majority of their knowledge-check answers (a rule set on 8 September 2026: pool with the models that answered). The count of such models is printed beside the rate, and the rate over every model is kept in the register under `.commission_all_models`. Every system with a panel run enters the pooled rows on the same footing; a case the bank's screen does not admit is still measured on the models that identified it, and the row says how many did.
