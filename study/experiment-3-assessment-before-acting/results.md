## The differences, with intervals

The table above prints two rates side by side and leaves the reader to subtract them. These are the subtractions, each with an interval of its own. Every contrast is computed on the models and cases the two arms share, and resampled together: one draw takes models with replacement, then cases with replacement inside each drawn model, and reads both arms on those same drawn models and cases before taking the difference. The arm columns are those same arms on that shared support, so the difference column is exactly the one minus the other. A negative number means the cell is lower than what it is measured against.
